Privacy Policy & Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Islamic Quotes & Duas is designed to provide inspirational Islamic quotes and beneficial reminders. We keep data collection minimal and do not sell personal information.

We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information (such as name, email, or phone number) unless you contact us directly.

The app may locally store preferences (for example: favorites, theme settings, onboarding completion) on your device for app functionality.

This app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect and process data according to Google policies, including device identifiers and ad interaction data.

For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect app data and avoid misuse. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we aim to follow practical security standards.

5. Child Safety Standards Policy

Islamic Quotes & Duas is strictly committed to maintaining a safe digital environment for all users. We enforce a zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) in any form.

  • Prohibited Conduct: Any action involving the creation, distribution, promotion, or facilitation of child sexual exploitation, abuse material, or grooming is strictly forbidden.
  • Reporting & Enforcement: We allow users to report safety concerns directly in-app. Any user account or content violating these standards will be terminated immediately, and illegal activities will be reported to relevant regional and national law enforcement authorities, including NCMEC.
  • Designated Point of Contact: If you have any child safety concerns, reports, or inquiries regarding our CSAE prevention practices, please contact our designated safety lead at: sr.nawaz446@gmail.com.

6. Children’s Privacy

The app is intended for general audiences. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us so we can review and remove it where applicable.
